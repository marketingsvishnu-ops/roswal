India's T20 World Cup triumph earns a record BCCI bonus

The cricket board announced a ₹131 crore reward for the title-winning squad after India's historic campaign.

By Sports DeskMarch 10, 2026Updated: March 10, 20263 min read
Cricket stadium atmosphere. Image used for illustrative purposes.

India's men's cricket team has received a major financial reward following its memorable T20 World Cup campaign. The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) announced a cash prize of ₹131 crore in recognition of the team's achievement.

The announcement followed India's victory over New Zealand in the final at Ahmedabad's Narendra Modi Stadium. The result added another milestone to India's record in the men's T20 World Cup.

At a glance
Announced reward: ₹131 crore
Presented by: Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI)
Occasion: India's 2026 men's T20 World Cup title

A reward beyond the tournament prize

The board's bonus was separate from the prize money awarded by the International Cricket Council for winning the tournament. It recognised the contribution of the players, coaching and support staff, and selectors throughout the campaign.

In its announcement, the BCCI congratulated the team on the achievement and wished the players and staff continued success. The reward also continued the board's practice of recognising major international title wins with additional cash awards.

Another milestone for Indian cricket

The victory strengthened India's place among the leading teams in the shortest international format. Attention will now turn to the team's next targets as it builds on the success of the World Cup campaign.
